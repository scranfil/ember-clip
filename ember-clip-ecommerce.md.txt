## About Ember Clip

The Ember Clip is a premium, purpose-built cigar accessory that solves the age-old problem: *where do I put my cigar when I need my hands?*

Engineered with a robust spring-loaded clamp and ultra-strong magnetic base, it keeps your cigar safe, elevated, and lit — whether you're driving a golf cart, steering a boat, or flipping burgers on the grill.

**Core Features:**
- Powerful neodymium magnets (holds up to 5 lbs on flat or curved metal)
- Adjustable soft-grip clamp fits cigars from small to 60+ ring gauge
- Durable, weather-resistant construction (vibrant blue)
- Compact, lightweight, and easy one-handed operation
- Designed and tested for real-world outdoor use
